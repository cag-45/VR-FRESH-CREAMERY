# Visual DNA & Design Specification — VR FRESH CREAMERY

## 1. Brand Essence & Logo Alignment
- **Brand Name:** VR FRESH CREAMERY
- **Tagline:** FRESH CREAM GELATO — Freshness in Every Scoop.
- **Brand Story:** An authentic artisanal creamery located in Medipalli, Medchal-Malkajgiri, crafting small-batch fresh-cream gelato using 100% whole farm milk and real seasonal fruits.
- **Design Philosophy:** Organic luxury, approachable freshness, non-AI-generated visual warmth. Matches the circular emblem logo with deep forest emerald tones, fresh leaf greens, and soft milk cream surfaces.

## 3. Typography Strategy (Bespoke Heritage + Artisan Script)
The brand logo combines a commanding serif with an intimate, hand-crafted script:
- **Serif Display (matches "VR"):** `Playfair Display` or `Fraunces` (weights: 700 / 900). Bold, authoritative, timeless.
- **Artisan Script (matches "Fresh Creamery"):** `Dancing Script` or `Caveat` (weights: 600 / 700). Handcrafted, warm, appetizing.
- **Category Eyebrow (matches "FRESH CREAM GELATO"):** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`text-xs uppercase tracking-[0.25em] font-bold text-[#0B4822]`).
- **Body & Microcopy:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights: 400 / 500 / 600) for clean mobile readability.
