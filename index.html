What Goes Into Your Spoon

A transparent look at how VR Fresh Creamery compares to typical mass-produced commercial ice cream.

The Dairy Base
VR Fresh Creamery

100% Pure Whole Farm Buffalo & Cow Milk with natural butterfat.

Commercial Frozen Dessert

Water, reconstituted skim milk powder, and hydrogenated palm oil (Vanaspati).

Fruit Content
VR Fresh Creamery

Real hand-deseeded custard apple, wild mulberries, and fresh tender coconut malai.

Commercial Frozen Dessert

Synthetic chemical flavorings, artificial colors (E102, E122), and sugary corn syrups.

Density & Air Ratio
VR Fresh Creamery

Slow batch-churned with minimal air (low overrun). Dense, spoon-coating, and rich.

Commercial Frozen Dessert

Pumped with 50% to 60% pressurized air so you are mostly buying cold air.

Freshness & Shelf Life
VR Fresh Creamery

Freshly churned every single morning. Kept pure and served at peak freshness.

Commercial Frozen Dessert

Loaded with chemical gums and stabilizers to survive 12 months in distributor freezers.
